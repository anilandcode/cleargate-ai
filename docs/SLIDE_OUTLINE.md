# ClearGate AI Slide Outline

## 1. Problem

- Employees adopt AI tools before governance review.
- Security, privacy, procurement, and GRC inherit a growing Shadow AI backlog.
- Evidence is scattered across vendor pages and changes over time.

## 2. Product Thesis

- ClearGate AI is the continuous approval gate for employee-adopted AI tools.
- Turn public evidence into explainable approval decisions and audit-ready handoff.

## 3. Workflow

Shadow AI Inbox -> Live Public Evidence -> Evidence Ledger -> Policy Gate -> Decision -> AI Vendor Passport -> Escalation Or Re-Review

## 4. Bright Data Infrastructure

- SERP API discovers canonical public sources.
- Web Unlocker retrieves selected official pages.
- Scraping Browser is a bounded optional escalation for dynamic pages.
- Provenance, excerpts, timestamps, and SHA-256 make evidence auditable.

## 5. Live Demo Proof

- Open Otter.ai from the populated inbox.
- Run live verification.
- Inspect one official source finding.
- Show deterministic policy explanation and export memo.

## 6. Differentiation

- Not a general-purpose research agent.
- Public evidence is connected to policy controls and business decisions.
- Optional AI/ML API extraction is grounded and non-authoritative.
- Slack package turns evidence into enterprise action.

## 7. Market And Business Value

- Buyer: security, privacy, procurement, TPRM, GRC, and AI governance.
- SaaS pricing by vendors, reviewer seats, and evidence review volume.
- Value: reduced triage effort, reusable audit evidence, faster approval cycles.

## 8. Closing

ClearGate AI helps enterprises adopt AI faster without losing control of evidence, accountability, or review quality.
